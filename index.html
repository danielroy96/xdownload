How to download X (Twitter) videos

xDownload is a free online X / Twitter video downloader. It saves videos and GIFs from any public post — in HD MP4, with no watermark, no login and no app to install. Three steps:

  1. Copy the post link. Open the X (Twitter) post with the video and copy its URL — it looks like https://x.com/username/status/….
  2. Paste it above. Drop the URL into the box and press Fetch. xDownload reads the post and lists every video it finds.
  3. Download or play. Press Download to save the highest-quality MP4, or play it right here in the page first.

Why use xDownload

  • HD MP4. Always grabs the highest-bitrate progressive video X offers.
  • No watermark. You get the original file, exactly as posted.
  • No login. No account, no sign-up, nothing to install.
  • Any device. Runs in the browser on iPhone, Android, tablet and desktop.
  • GIFs too. Twitter GIFs are really MP4s — xDownload saves them the same way.

Frequently asked questions

Is xDownload free to use?

Yes. xDownload is completely free with no account, no sign-up and no software to install. Just paste a post URL and download.

Do downloaded videos have a watermark?

No. xDownload saves the original video file from X (Twitter) exactly as posted, with no watermark added.

What quality can I download?

xDownload always picks the highest-bitrate progressive MP4 available for the post, so you get the best quality X offers for that video.

Can I download Twitter GIFs too?

Yes. Animated GIFs on X are really MP4 videos, and xDownload detects and downloads them the same way as normal videos.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. xDownload runs entirely in the browser and works on iPhone, Android, tablets and desktop — no app required.
