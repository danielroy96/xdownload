Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

xDownload (“we”, “us”) lets you extract and download publicly available videos from X (formerly Twitter) posts. This page explains what data we handle and how cookies are used on this site.

What we collect

We do not require an account and we do not ask you for personal information. The post URL you paste is used only to look up that post’s media and is not stored by us. We keep a single value in your browser’s local storage (cookieNoticeAck) to remember that you’ve seen the cookie notice.

Third-party services

To provide the service we send the post URL to third parties on your behalf:

  • fxtwitter — used to retrieve the post’s media information.
  • Our own Cloudflare Worker proxy — used to re-serve video files so they can be downloaded in one click. Cloudflare may process request metadata (such as IP address) as part of delivering and protecting the service.

Advertising & cookies

This site displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance.

You can review and control how Google uses data for advertising at Google Ads & cookies, and opt out of personalised ads at Google Ad Settings. For more choices, see aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu (EU/UK).

For details on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see the Google partner-sites policy.

Content & responsibility

xDownload is a tool for accessing publicly available media. You are solely responsible for the content you choose to download and for how you use it, including respecting copyright, the rights of the original poster, and X’s terms of service. We do not host, own, or moderate this content and accept no responsibility or liability for it.

We do not intentionally track, log, or retain the posts or files you download. The URLs you paste are used only to fulfil your request in the moment and are not stored by us. (As noted above, third parties such as Cloudflare may process request metadata to deliver and protect the service.)

Your choices

You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may mean you see less relevant ads but does not affect the core download functionality.
